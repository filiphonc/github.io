WHO I AM

I'm a motivated young individual with a strong interest in technology, mechanics, and adrenaline sports. I approach everything with a hands-on mindset — whether that's debugging Java code at 2am or digging into the engine bay of my BMW E39.

My world is driven by curiosity and persistence. I'm the kind of person who takes things apart to understand how they work, then puts them back together — usually better than before.

I believe that software and mechanical engineering share the same DNA: systems, logic, feedback loops, and the patience to iterate. Whether I'm designing a class hierarchy in Java or planning a speaker wiring diagram, the mindset is the same.

Beyond the technical, I'm drawn to speed — downhill mountain biking pushes my limits in ways that coding can't quite replicate. And when I slow down, it's to play guitar or plan my next trip somewhere most people haven't been.

Build fast. Break things. Fix them better.
Then push harder.
